## Review and access contract

- **Administrator:** explicitly opens a projected source case, assigns/revokes investigation, reads impact/history, continues/closes without change, or independently approves a supported proposal. A proposal/technical-finding author cannot approve its relaxation, including after a role change. Independence is enforced by different accountable user IDs; real-person identity and technical competence are not independently verified.
- **Processor:** must be active, explicitly assigned, currently linked to an approved capable organization/place, with exact subject category/subtype capability. Supplies actual observation provenance, same-subject code/basis, proposed controlled fact, reason, private evidence/reference and YES/NO/UNKNOWN attestations. Assignment alone proves no physical observation.
- **Pre-receipt investigation:** requires explicit case/subject/named-Processor grant under existing capable scope. No accounts or links are manufactured. Only controlled code/fact/case information and specifically granted source evidence are exposed; no general search, private serial, owner contact/address or unrelated photos. Existing legitimate custody/place scope can be reused through explicit assignment. Membership/place/operator/capability drift, revocation and case closure remove technical access, including old file links.
- **Original observer:** current scoped original Collector/point staff may clarify their own receipt comparison; clarification grants no proposal/approval/receipt authority.
- **Consumer:** sees only owned safe review milestones. Staff reasons, evidence, source events, technical identity basis, other owners and private measurements are withheld. Case notices remain generic and reauthorize their destination link.

Private reasons, basis and documentary references are encrypted. Source identifiers are opaque references; the QR/manual code is a physical comparison reference, never bearer authorization. Case files use the existing private evidence service and download sandbox; investigators cannot read another author's private case evidence merely by obtaining its UUID. Administrators retain accountable archive access after closure; investigator/clarifier access ends.

## Sources, states and effective facts

`restriction_sources` projects existing ledger facts. Reads create no historical cases/events. The source registry retains immutable source records even after a separate supported workflow changes their current blocking status; the queue shows current blockers. Explicit commands alone create cases and assignment. One active case per exact source record/individual fact prevents competing investigations of the same fact.

Case operation history is append-only: open, assign/revoke, original clarification, note, technical proposal, continue, close, approve. **Closed without change** retains the underlying blocker. **Superseded** means one independently evidenced recorded fact was superseded and fresh workflow prerequisites are required. A newer source or dependency stales an older proposal. Reassignment requires new findings under the new grant; restoring old scope does not revive its old version.

Supported inspection facts cover shared/secondary safety concern, charging/battery/thermal concern, media identification and fan voltage/size/application/electronics. Replacement values are explicitly known: NO for a concern, YES for supported media identity, WITHIN/ICT/BASIC for corresponding fan facts; receipt correction records an erroneous original comparison, not a new receipt. Every proposal must attest direct observation, same subject, erroneous recording, then-and-now consistency, no remediation/movement/configuration change and known supported fact. Non-YES or UNKNOWN cannot obtain positive approval. Independent source facts remain effective.

`effective_restrictions` and database functions provide the common PHP/SQL contract. Original discrepancies, answers, recommendations, hard-hold flags and decisions are never edited. Approved reviews create exact supported supersessions and consecutive immutable epoch steps/current epochs for connected subject/media/batch dependencies. Workflow stamps are captured by database triggers only on new records, including savepoint/root transactions; old history is not backfilled with fresh authority.

Receipt eligibility, inspection decisions, media inventory/authorization/applicability, sanitization assurance, reuse/work tests, batch gates and downstream blockers consume that contract. A new Ready review/allocation/plan or new inspection/human decision is required. Old work/release/media authority cannot revive; justified applicability review still uses the existing assurance ledger and unchanged method policy. No extra repeat physical work is invented.

Completed work/physical/terminal dependencies remain unchanged and acquire append-only blocking impact exceptions where review affects their context. These impacts have no C14 resolution. Clearing the specific original source therefore does not imply that all dependent progression is permitted. Mass, stock, custody, ownership, first receipts, original evidence and terminal outcomes remain authoritative.
