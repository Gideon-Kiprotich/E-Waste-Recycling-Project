## Design deliverables and decisions

[The Phase 18 design](phase-18-design.md) maps FR33/BR20/UC18/US20/S16 and applicable AT/NF contracts to nine typed actions (PA01–PA09), seventeen actual data-surface groups (DI01–DI17), eleven retention classes (RT01–RT11) and twelve planned verification groups (PV01–PV12). The requirements audit records dependencies without marking design as implementation.

Source review covered the approved D2/D3 policy, C18 audit, current notice and locally reviewed S16 HTML/image; auth/session/reset/logging/filesystem code; immutable identity/events/commands; private upload/evidence conventions; request/directory/assignment/routing copies; technical/review contexts; and C17 timeline/notice/export metadata and bytes. Only source/schema definitions and the specifically requested retained verification evidence were inspected. No live account/request values, environment/key contents, private images/documents, application-log contents or real backup collection were opened for the inventory.

Important proposed boundaries:

- Own-only access for the four existing roles is a proposed extension of UC18's Consumer/Admin reference flow, with current authentication/fresh confirmation and no cross-owner staff evidence grant. Unsupported assisted/email verification remains pending.
- Personal name/contact changes are versioned personal revisions. Canonical serial, custody, quantity, source date, inspection and ownership changes remain existing supported operational correction work or explicitly unsupported identity policy.
- Irreversible work needs independent approval, exact resource/field plans and native reciprocal guards; availability denial precedes erasure. Partial file/register/DB failure resumes monotonically and never fabricates completion, actor identity or proof.
- Clear identifier removal does not silently clear global uniqueness. Recommended restricted comparison HMAC/lineage retention ends at an approved study cutoff, remains pseudonymous and is disclosed as partial erasure. Active-namespace HMAC erasure needs a separate identity-policy decision.
- The package contract excludes credentials/keys/other owners/private staff proof and binds all generation/downloads to current own scope/session/privacy context. Caps, expiry, combined quota and explicit partition coverage are proposed.
- Retention distinguishes existing A6/C17 obligations from proposed temporary, audit, receipt and backup schedules. Current notice wording is not silently weakened. No statutory retention period or legal-compliance claim is invented.
- Backups remain retained until actual expiry. A durable independent selector register, versioned joint DB/blob/snapshot replay and a quarantine/release barrier prevent old copies/session authority from becoming accessible. Real backup setup, failure-domain durability and restore targets remain unverified.

Five concrete groups A1–A5 require explicit authorization and append-only D2/D3 clarification before code: identity/actors and closure access; typed correction/redaction guards; comparison/lineage retention and retirement; package/retention profile; durable backup/register/replay policy. The design makes these choices reviewable now instead of asking for approval of unspecified implementation.
