# Phase 10A assurance contract — frozen before implementation, 16 October 2026

Authority: D2 FR23, BR09/BR14/BR15/BR19/BR20; D3 UC11, S11, AT05/AT06/AT08/AT10/AT11; CR-ID-01 and CR-PARTS-01. This increment records external treatment of existing canonical standalone HDD/SSD storage facets only. It neither performs treatment nor authorizes release. A current non-HOLD human pathway decision is the user-accepted project default, not an additional claim about UC11's original precondition. Any currently authorized facility Processor may perform each separate recording/review action; different people are not required.

## Sources verified before implementation

[NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2, September 2025](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-88r2.pdf), §§3, 4.5–4.6 and Appendix B: verification examines execution results; validation decides adequacy in context. Media, coverage, errors, equipment and confidentiality risks matter. Destruction is incompatible with reuse; degaussing does not sanitize flash. A certificate documents assertions and does not independently establish effectiveness. Rev. 2 supersedes Rev. 1. No certification or universal efficacy is claimed here.

[Seagate official deletion matrix](https://www.seagate.com/support/seagate-support-us/) and [manufacturer Sanitize guidance](https://github.com/Seagate/openSeaChest/wiki/Sanitize-Feature), retrieved 16 October 2026: support varies by model/firmware/interface; completion and coverage evidence are needed. The matrix classifies sanitize overwrite as clear and SSD block erase as purge. These sources inform the narrow project policy below; they do not establish support for an arbitrary drive. No destructive commands are reproduced or executed.

## Frozen project policy MEDIA-ASSURANCE-1.0

- `hdd_sanitize_overwrite`: CLEAR, HDD only. Record manufacturer-supported firmware sanitize overwrite, full target scope, tool/version and procedure/model capability evidence. Hybrid/unknown media and uncertain inaccessible areas cannot be accepted by assumption.
- `ssd_sanitize_block_erase`: PURGE, SSD only. Record supported firmware block erase, tool/version, procedure/model capability and coverage evidence, including relevant non-addressable areas. HDD overwrite is not an SSD clearance shortcut.
- `media_shredding`: DESTROY, HDD or SSD. Record equipment identity and procedure (including the applicable media-specific destruction criterion and observed result). No invented universal particle-size threshold; adequacy must be justified by the accountable validator. Software version is inapplicable; equipment identity/procedure remain mandatory.
- `other_clear`, `other_purge`, `other_destroy`, `unknown`: honest record-only classifications. Unsupported in policy 1.0, including cryptographic erase/degaussing/formatting, regardless of an uploaded certificate or reported success. `other_destroy` also retains non-reusability concerns. No N/A for these permanently YES media.

All reported successes require executor, scope, procedure, tool/equipment and evidence/reference; logical successes also require tool version. Failed/incomplete/unverifiable records permit unknown technique and omitted unavailable metadata/evidence, with a reason. Observed time remains actual: if execution time is unknown for negative work, record when the failure/report was observed and explain the unknown execution time, rather than inventing it. All supplied private text is encrypted. Explicit structured verification/validation answers use YES/NO/UNKNOWN; positive outcomes require every applicable answer YES plus private review evidence and rationale.

## State and selection contract

Each subject has one independent consecutive sanitization version and immutable predecessor chain. Physical subject, custody, pathway, processing state, reservations and collection counts are unchanged. Freeze subject, inspection/decision, custody, facility/operator and recording-access versions on every action. The current pointer references the newest action and its attempt; it cannot select an older success. Identical successful command retries return their original record after current authorization, before stale/gate checks, without restoring that record as current. Changed retries conflict.

1. No attempt → **Required**. Record an attempt → **Pending verification** for success, otherwise **Failed / Incomplete / Unverifiable**. Every new attempt supersedes earlier clearance, references the preceding attempt and preserves all history.
2. **Record verification** is a separate command against the current reported-success attempt; only supported work can receive positive assurance. Confirm identity/scope, execution completion and absence of unresolved errors/anomalies (including remnants/equipment for destruction). Satisfactory → **Pending validation**; unsatisfactory/unverifiable → blocked, new physical attempt needed. An attachment alone cannot verify.
3. **Record validation** separately references satisfactory verification under the same dependencies. Confirm technique/media appropriateness, coverage/evidence adequacy and acceptable residual risk. Accepted → **Data treatment satisfied (attested)**; rejected → **Rejected**, new attempt needed. No release or reuse suitability is inferred.
4. Current dependency drift, absent decision, revoked/incompatible facility or blocking hold → **Applicability review required**, even if historical validation was accepted. Restore legitimate prerequisites first. A previously accepted latest attempt can use **Revalidate applicability**: explicitly review prior verification/evidence against fresh dependencies, attest unchanged controlled media and continuing adequacy, and accept/reject with new evidence/reason. This is an applicability review, not new physical work. Unassured attempts instead need fresh verification/validation; stale reviews never silently count. Rejected/negative evidence cannot be undone by revalidation; append a real new attempt.
5. Any reported destruction attempt permanently marks **Destruction reported — non-reusable** (including uncertain/failed destruction, whose damage cannot be disproved by a later ordinary success). Only a supported destruction attempt can thereafter satisfy data treatment; logical success cannot erase that concern. Verified destruction and accepted destruction are separately labelled. Even accepted destruction never records disposal, dismantling, closure or reuse suitability.

Whole devices display **Media inventory support pending**; fans are outside this media workflow. Neither can receive a fake N/A/clearance. Phase 10B must settle complete embedded inventory, canonical identity/provenance and controlled detachment before claiming whole-device coverage.

## Expected cases fixed before implementation

- HDD/overwrite and SSD/block erase: success alone blocked; satisfactory verification alone blocked; separate accepted validation satisfies only data treatment. Media remains YES and the existing facet/identity/custody/history stay identical.
- Crossed media techniques, unknown/other methods, missing success metadata, non-YES assurance answers and a certificate alone cannot satisfy the gate. Honest negative attempts remain recordable.
- Negative verification, validation rejection, newer failed/incomplete/unverifiable attempt and reported destruction prevent fallback to an older success. New attempts preserve predecessor links.
- Reassessment, decision/custody/facility/operator/access drift require review. Explicit accepted revalidation of still-applicable latest accepted evidence may restore data treatment without another physical attempt; newer negative evidence or a hard hold forbids it.
- Same command/same payload returns its historical result; changed payload/stale competing command conflicts. Real concurrent writers yield one winner. Deferred reciprocal constraints must be forced, not merely rolled back untested.
- Cross-facility/revoked access, whole-device posts, forged fields/current pointers, detached evidence and incomplete event/command chains are denied. Rollback removes new unreferenced files; old evidence and unrelated outbox rows remain intact.
- Fresh and populated isolated upgrades preserve all prior rows and files, introducing only the frozen policy, with zero invented treatment records. Development prerequisites are inspected, never manufactured.

Manual browser/bench, mobile/keyboard/screen-reader, real operator suitability and Nairobi UTC-entry usability remain deferred. This policy and its tests demonstrate application behavior, not real treatment effectiveness or complete UC11 acceptance.
