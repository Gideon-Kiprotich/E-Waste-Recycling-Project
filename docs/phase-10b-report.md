## Identity, provenance and inventory decisions

Processor-only `processor_media` / `processor_derived` supports `hdd`, `ssd`, `other_nonvolatile`. Its category is explicitly unavailable to ordinary supported Consumer/directory intake; existing HDD/SSD handling capabilities are reused for derived treatment. Each identified medium has one permanent item/subject/QR identity and same-ID storage facet, permanent YES, optional encrypted serial and unchanged global comparison namespace/index. No parent serial is copied. Physical identity is created only after affirmative physical matching, ownership consistency and safe-observation checks plus distinguishing placement/evidence. New accountability ownership derives from the evidenced parent; no ownership transfer is asserted.

`media_origins` records the first actual facility identification and anchors it to the parent's accepted facility custody. Derived current control begins there at custody version one, without a fictitious first receipt, handover, consumer registration/self-report, earlier transfer, removal or child assessment. Existing first-receipt/inspection/handover guards remain intact; a new reciprocal derived-origin guard validates the explicit alternative. Derived identities remain inventoried and cannot independently enter collection/transfer workflows. Existing standalone identity recognition is restricted to the same owner and current facility, an accepted eligible handover and no conflicting claim/restriction. No unrestricted identifier search/adoption exists; matching serial alone grants nothing.

Immutable `media_inventory_records` advances a separate parent inventory aggregate; `media_inventory_current` and `media_inventory_entries` are minimal ledger-backed projections. Uncertain presence/count/matching creates stable unbound entries, not physical subjects. Resolving an unresolved entry preserves its ID. Every medium is exclusively claimed by one parent through a database unique key; a disputed claim remains exclusive. Standalone origins remain unchanged and parent-null; observed placement is separate from origin.

**Corrections are bounded:** unbound observations may remain unresolved or become evidenced not-present. A bound identity can only be marked disputed, retaining its subject, placement claim and all treatment/destruction history. No omission, deletion, merge or reassignment clears a canonical obligation. Resolving an incorrectly bound canonical identity still requires separately reviewed correction functionality; this limitation is visible in the UI. Actual removal/replacement cannot be disguised as an observation correction.

Completeness YES requires all explicit checks YES (all areas accounted, configuration storage included, identity/count resolved, no concealed physical change), private evidence and no unresolved/disputed entries. NO/UNKNOWN remains valid blocked inventory. N/A additionally requires current technician NO, no identified applicable medium, complete current inventory and a separate explicit encrypted justification. Empty lists and Consumer declarations alone establish nothing.

## Authorization, assurance and coverage

Immutable consecutive `media_authorizations` and its current pointer bind derived HDD/SSD to parent inventory/subject/assessment/decision, coherent parent/media custody, facility/operator/access/session versions, media-specific safety/scope assertions, time and private evidence. A current non-HOLD parent decision and explicit HDD/SSD facility capability are required. Unknown safety checks or unresolved holds block authorization. Existing standalone drives retain their own assessment/decision path; parent authorization cannot substitute for it. Other nonvolatile media is inventory-only and shows **Treatment support unavailable**.

The original `sanitization_records` / `sanitization_current` ledger is reused. Additive authorization context 1 retains standalone semantics; context 2 explicitly references the derived parent authorization and freezes the parent's assessment/decision as such, never a fabricated child assessment. Placement reference freezes applicability for recognized standalone media too. MEDIA-ASSURANCE-1.0 and its checksum/resource are unchanged. Attempts, verification, validation and applicability revalidation remain separate commands; no certificate or inventory attestation creates treatment success.

One authoritative database `media_coverage` calculation combines latest current inventory/inspection and every identified medium's live assurance. It reports concrete missing/incomplete, unresolved/disputed, unsupported, treatment pending/failed/rejected, stale and held reasons. Technician UNKNOWN blocks; technician NO conflicts with identified storage. Inventory/assessment/context drift invalidates affected coverage/authorization. Fresh attestation/authorization and legitimate explicit applicability review preserve old evidence without fabricating new physical work. Negative/newer treatment never falls back to older success.

Data treatment and reuse are separate. Destruction remains permanently recorded on the canonical medium and parent coverage shows the concern even after a disputed relationship. Coverage always returns reuse unauthorized. No inventory correction can establish a replacement physical configuration. Independent embedded-media movement and custody-changing parent actions are blocked with a controlled-detachment/co-transfer explanation. Existing facility redistribution is already outside supported handovers; database guards also reject direct movement while media is claimed. No implicit paired custody change occurs.

## Exact-label manual walkthroughs — deferred, not passed

1. **Parent with two supported media:** prepare approved facility/operator, exact parent and HDD/SSD capabilities and scoped Processor; complete real facility receipt and **Assessments / Inspection** observations/current non-HOLD decision. Open **Media inventory → Record inventory observation**, identify each actual medium with distinguishing placement/evidence, leaving unknown serials blank. **Attest completeness** including configuration storage. **Authorize media treatment → Authorize [EWC code]** for each derived drive, then its **Open media assurance and history → Record external attempt → Record verification → Record validation**. **Review coverage** must require both. Use synthetic records, never real wiping/destruction for application acceptance.
2. **Existing standalone identity:** an actually matching standalone drive must already belong to the same owner under accepted current facility control with its own current inspection/decision. Use **Recognize existing medium → Known medium label / manual code**, supply accountable physical-match evidence. It retains its original code/identity/owner/history. After **Attest completeness**, use its own **Revalidate applicability** where eligible; do not use parent-based authorization.
3. **Unresolved inventory:** **Record unresolved inventory** for uncertain count/presence/matching. Inspect the stable entry and blocked coverage. Resolve an unbound entry using **Record inventory observation → Existing inventory entry** or **Recognize existing medium**. Use **Correct inventory entry** only for evidenced observation correction; a bound mistake becomes disputed and stays blocked/claimed.
4. **Other nonvolatile storage:** **Record inventory observation → Other nonvolatile storage — inventory only**, with actual distinguishing evidence. Inspect **Treatment support unavailable** and blocked coverage. No HDD/SSD technique or category-based exemption appears.
5. **Evidenced N/A:** current technician assessment must independently establish NO. **Attest completeness** with all affirmative checks, private evidence and **Explicit N/A justification** establishing no applicable storage. Empty/uncertain/inaccessible inventory or an identified medium must not produce N/A.
6. **Reassessment/inventory change:** retain older evidence; inspect blocked/stale coverage, record fresh **Attest completeness**, then fresh **Authorize media treatment** for derived drives. Follow **Open media assurance and history → Revalidate applicability → Record applicability revalidation** only for still-applicable latest accepted work. Negative/rejected evidence needs a real new external attempt; never manufacture repeated work or use this route to clear holds/destruction.
