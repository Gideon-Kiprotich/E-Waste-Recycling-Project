Governance • Pol-04

Statutory Privacy Notice (v3.2)

Gazetted 2024
Legal Notice Scope: Effective 1st January 2024 pursuant to Section 25 of the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and Regulation 14 of the EMCA (Waste Management) Regulations.

1. Personal Identifiers vs. Immutable Physical Scrap Lineage

Consignor names, phone numbers, and physical residential coordinates are strictly pseudonymized and stored in Tier 4 encrypted silos. In contrast, non-identifying physical parameters—specifically hardware net weight, battery chemistry classifications, CRT lead mass, and precious metal recovery yields—are recorded onto an irreversible municipal ledger to ensure chain-of-custody compliance under EMCA 2006.

2. Right to Erasure & Municipal Retention Limits

A registered consignor may exercise their KDPA Right to Erasure regarding personal profile credentials. However, statutory consignment manifests, custody transfer tokens, and hazardous waste destruction certificates remain permanent county public records to prevent illicit dumping in the Nairobi River Basin.

3. Data Controller Contact

Office of the County Data Protection Officer, City Hall, P.O. Box 30075-00100 Nairobi. Designated Registry Email: dpo@nairobi.go.ke.
