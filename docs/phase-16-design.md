## 3. Shared review, evidence and access contract

Use existing S14 case opening/explicit assignment, technical findings, proposal, independent Administrator review, continue/close-without-change, private evidence, commands/events/outbox and typed continuity/fulfillment. Add contract P16 and closed action templates; keep C14/P15 semantics and successful historical retries. No second exception framework or public maintenance endpoint.

An active Administrator coordinates and approves. The reviewer must differ from every technical/finding/proposal author and every responsibility/receipt signature relied upon, even after role changes. Technical findings require explicit case assignment and current approved **actual source or destination facility** Processor scope, exact output acceptance profile/capability, and HDD/SSD subtype capability where applicable. A pre-receipt grant does not confer downstream authority. Source staff cannot attest destination possession; destination staff cannot establish source stock from a dispatch slip alone.

When both facilities' facts change, collect separate signed findings from each: one lead case investigator plus linked party findings, all current at approval. An original reporter can clarify their own record under current limited scope; revoked staff are not reactivated to finish a case. An original accountable issuer/reference can support investigation without conferring an inactive person's action authority. If required evidence or an eligible actual recipient is unavailable, continue investigation/hold; do not insert a proxy receipt.

Every action freezes: exact source event/record/fact and effective revision; batch/output/shipment/claim IDs; original and proposed controlled values and complete quantity vector; original measurement/provenance; original fact time, new observation time and server recording time; then-and-now identity/control facts; reason plus private attachment or accountable issuer/reference; case, restriction, downstream, stock, recipient-generation, facility/profile, inventory and assurance versions. No guessed serials or private evidence in events, logs, flash input or URLs. Proposed change must prove an error **at the original time**, or explicitly record a later real condition/incident instead. UNKNOWN never supports relaxation.

Technical observation may truthfully predate case creation but not its original source; later findings respect the source/case bounds. Retain separate historical fact time, current observation and review recording time instead of backdating an approval or replacing observation with server time. Fresh actual acknowledgment records its own actor/time/evidence.

Recheck all cited signatures, current roles/control, evidence provenance and exact dependencies under the existing transaction gate and stable ordered locks at approval and deferred COMMIT. Restoring a role/profile later does not revive an old signature. Affected live authority becomes stale; independently completed facts receive exact obligations. Approval alone never means stock returned, accepted, treated, recovered or released.

Private access: source Processor sees own production/movement facts and only necessary recipient outcome references; destination Processor sees their shipment/quantity/identity and relevant receiving evidence, not all batch members/owners or source private inventory. An explicitly assigned cross-party investigator gets allowlisted case evidence only, not new general facility access. Independent Admin sees the case/impact evidence under current role. Obsolete recipients lose actionable links and private attachments; historical authorship remains. Consumers see only their own source association and safe aggregate status, never mixed-owner membership, kg, evidence, recipient personal details or per-device recovery percentages. All old links reauthorize live.

## 4. Exact accounting model

All kg use numeric(12,3), integer grams (one unit = 0.001 kg) in PHP and numeric arithmetic in PostgreSQL; reuse [Mass](../app/Support/Batches/Mass.php). Reject negatives, overflow, exponent notation, excess precision, inconsistent gross/tare/net and implicit rounding. Unknown is NULL/pending, never zero. Zero may retire a proven erroneous exception/loss/outcome claim only through review, not create a zero physical movement or negate an actual acknowledgment. Retained production, physical dispatch and actual accepted allocations require positive mass; a canonical unit cannot become zero mass to release its identity claim.

For each original output, at one effective revision:

- P = effective evidenced mass of that **same original production**, not extra production. D = total effective dispatched kg. Ls = effective confirmed source loss. R = outstanding reservations (reserved minus withdrawn minus dispatched). Source quantity A = P − D − Ls. Available V = A − R. Require P ≥ 0, D ≥ 0, Ls ≥ 0, R ≥ 0, A ≥ 0 and V ≥ 0. Pending/unmeasured production is not reservable.
- Reservation withdrawal q: R decreases by q; V increases by q; P, D, Ls and A are unchanged. It releases no dispatched material. Dispatch q: D increases and R decreases by q; A decreases once and V is unchanged at that step. Receipt/completion/downstream loss change neither D nor source A/V. Source loss q decreases A/V once, and only from unreserved available aggregate quantity.
- For each actual dispatch d, partition its effective quantity exactly: Dd = Ad + Fd + Sd + Qd + Ltd + Ud. A is acknowledged accepted; F refused but not returned; S shortage pending investigation; Q disputed; Lt reviewed/recorded transit loss; U unreported remainder. These are disjoint claim slices, not six independent totals each allowed to consume all kg. All are nonnegative; unknown outcome remains in U/S/Q/F, not presumed loss/recovery. A reviewed false report frees only its exact slice for a fresh actual acknowledgment or a separately evidenced outcome.
- For each actual acceptance a: Aa = Cr + Cd + Cs + La + Pa. Cr = evidenced recovered; Cd = evidenced disposed; Cs = supported controlled storage; La = reviewed confirmed **post-acceptance** loss; P = outstanding final outcome. Damaged is a condition/hold on the accepted slice, not another kg bucket. Every completion consumes accepted pending quantity only. Post-acceptance loss consumes that pending quantity, not dispatch capacity again. A later loss contradicting already completed quantity first opens a dependent exception; it cannot overlap the retained effective completion.
- Total downstream loss is Lt + La; it is already inside D and is never subtracted again from source stock, counted as recovered or inferred as physical return. Report recovery only from current valid Cr evidence; storage, disposal and loss stay distinct. Batch input mass includes embedded drives once; output media are never new inputs.

Canonical drives are **one indivisible physical unit**, never fractional kg allocation: one production/output, at most one physical dispatch, one actual destination custody acceptance and one terminal/accountability partition. Its measured kg can be corrected only with the same complete unit consistently represented across affected allocations (§5D). No second dispatch is enabled by reducing its effective kg. Owner/label/identifier remain unchanged.

Historical and effective values must both be visible. Original rows remain immutable; append reviewed fact revisions with exact predecessor and aggregate revision/approval ID. A later correction can supersede the previous *effective fact revision*, never erase an earlier review or apply additive deltas twice. Claims retain original dispatch/acceptance ancestry and disjointness. One shared effective resolver serves SQL/PHP stock, remaining quantities, inbox, readiness, assurance, closure and future C17 reports; do not leave raw SUM callers granting authority beside corrected views.

Mass balance remains input minus measured outputs, with unchanged prototype tolerance max(0.100 kg, 2% of measured input). Source/downstream loss is a stock/outcome fact, not a balancing output that repairs production variance. Honest imbalance can be recorded as evidence and stays blocking. No assumed yield, invented loss, silently cleared discrepancy or normalization to a desired total.

## 5. P16 action matrix

Each action inherits §3 evidence/versions/privacy and §4 equations. The following named states are proposed **effective review states**, not rewrites of historical operation kinds. References are explicit per action; AT10/AT11 concurrency/history applies throughout. Closing an investigation without change always leaves the original blocker effective.

### A — Correct an erroneous refusal, shortage or quantity-dispute report

**References/blocker:** FR25–FR29; BR05–BR06/BR11–BR14; UC14/UC16, S12/S14, AT06/AT09. Existing refuse/shortage/dispute consumes a dispatch slice and permanently blocks downstream progression.

**Eligibility/authority:** exact original uncompleted disputed slice, same output/destination, known same identity/control; no actual return or changed destination. Destination assigned Processor investigates their report, with source findings if dispatch facts are contested; independent Admin approves. A separate current named recipient, not Admin, supplies any outstanding physical acknowledgment.

**Evidence/effects:** original report, then-time delivery/count/weight/label evidence, current whereabouts/control and proposed erroneous fraction. Retire/reduce only proven erroneous F/S/Q into U; V/D/custody/production are unchanged. Proven real missing or refused material cannot use this action. A shortage that actually occurred then was later found is a new observed outcome, not proof that the earlier shortage report was false; retain its truth and append the later located/available evidence and reviewed transition to awaiting actual acknowledgment at the same destination. This narrow outcome continuation requires no return/reconsignment and no conflicting receipt/control.

**Fresh steps/closure:** show awaiting actual acknowledgment; named recipient scans/manual-checks and attests the exact available quantity. Only that action can consume U into A/change canonical custody once. Fresh completion evidence follows separately. A true refusal still physically unresolved remains held. If no supported known outcome exists, do not clear it merely because a case was approved. Post-closure reports follow §7.

**Invariants/verification:** exact slice predecessor, no concurrent double release/ack, no restock or disguised receipt; test partial aggregates, whole drives, true-versus-false/located shortage, denied unknowns/different destination, stale report and competing acknowledgments (DR01).

### B — Review damaged accepted material and supported disposition

**References/blocker:** FR26/FR28–FR29; BR06/BR09/BR12/BR14/BR17; UC14/UC16, S12/S14 with S11 for media, AT05–AT06/AT09. An accept with condition damaged retains real custody but ordinary complete is denied.

**Eligibility/authority:** exact accepted slice at its same authorized destination, current known safe scope and no actual return/replacement. Destination assigned capable Processor records damage nature, then/current condition, hazard/data implications and supported proposed disposition; independent Admin reviews. The current named recipient remains responsible for actual completion evidence.

**Effects:** distinguish (1) proven erroneous condition flag from (2) genuine damage with a reviewed safe disposition. Preserve raw damaged acceptance in both. Neither changes A/D/source stock/custody or manufactures recovered kg. For non-data aggregate material/component/residue with known NONE hazard and exact current profile, allow a reviewed plan for separately evidenced recovered/disposed work within existing output-type policy (residue only disposed). Review is not completion. Unknown hazards, unsupported remediation and unverifiable treatment stay held.

**Media boundary:** a mistaken damage report can recover only with same-medium evidence, fresh safety/applicability and live assurance. Genuine damaged HDD/SSD remain held unless the unchanged supported policy and evidenced safe intact identity can actually establish controlled storage; no repair, destruction, disposal clearance or reuse is authorized here. Destruction concern is permanent, not a condition flag to clear. Existing unsupported hard restrictions require their own supported review, never this case's blanket approval.

**Fresh steps/closure:** retain damaged historical label and effective disposition review, then require the recipient's actual evidenced completion against pending A. Review alone cannot satisfy closure. Safe storage is not recovery or release. Loss after acceptance follows C, not a new source deduction. Verify damaged versus error, residue/material outcomes, hazards, withheld media clearance and current recipient/scope loss (DR02).

### C — Investigate source-stock or downstream loss, including proven reporting error

**References/blocker:** FR25–FR29; BR06/BR09/BR11–BR14/BR19; UC14/UC16, S12/S14, AT06/AT09. Source_loss/downstream_loss always block closure today; source_loss currently supports aggregates only.

**Authority/evidence:** source assigned Processor for source incidents; current named destination Processor for dispatched/accepted incidents; source findings for dispatch ancestry; independent Admin. Require exact original output/claim, quantified incident basis, location/last confirmed responsibility, time, identity for media, search/accountability evidence and clear original/proposed outcome. A shortage alone is insufficient proof of loss.

**C1 Proven erroneous loss:** reduce/retire the exact false loss claim. False source loss can increase V only where evidence proves the same quantity never left source control and is physically present/unallocated now. A later physical return is excluded; an Admin incident closure cannot restock it. False downstream loss returns the exact quantity to U (before receipt) or Pa (after valid receipt), never to source availability. Require fresh actual receipt if outstanding, or new completion evidence if already accepted; preserve prior valid custody. Unknown current presence remains unresolved even if the old number was wrong.

**C2 Confirmed aggregate loss:** retain existing deduction/allocation once, append terminal lost_not_recovered accountability review, never a completion/recovery receipt. Before-receipt loss consumes dispatch U or the reviewed reclassification of S/Q/F; after-acceptance loss consumes Pa. A real source loss consumes V; withdraw relevant R through ordinary controls first. No inference from the source being unable to contact a receiver. Identified evidence must distinguish confirmed quantified loss from unexplained missing/unknown outcome.

**C3 Canonical media incident:** permit recording the **whole known unit** as a source incident under an explicit new typed path, or transit/accepted incident, without a fractional source-loss adjustment. Retain last confirmed custody row as history plus a current unavailable/uncontrolled incident state; never assert controlled storage, receipt, successful treatment or destruction. Block movement, assurance applicability, reuse and closure. Prior successful treatment remains historical evidence, not proof of current control or new data clearance. A false incident can recover only by demonstrated same-unit continuous control; actually recovered/returned/moved media need excluded physical workflows, not incident supersession.

**Closure:** recommend allowing an otherwise reconciled aggregate-only batch's confirmed quantified reviewed loss as a distinct non-recovery terminal outcome, with zero source R/A, every other dispatch/accept slice accounted, incident accountability evidence and no independent hold. Unknown whereabouts of **media**, unknown identities/data risk or unresolved fractions prevent safe current batch closure. Keep media accountability/data obligations open; incident investigation may close without clearing them. Historical closed batches remain historical with current exception status. No unsupported destruction certificate can solve this.

**Invariants/verification:** Lt/La/Ls disjoint, no second deduction, no fictitious recovery/return, whole-unit media incident exclusion from clearance, approval independence and fresh current presence. Exercise errors versus real loss, before/after acceptance, residual stock, full loss, concurrent loss/dispatch and post-completion contradiction (DR03).

### D — Correct evidenced measurement/accounting facts after dispatch

**References/blocker:** FR25–FR29; BR11–BR15/BR19; UC14/UC16, S12/S14, AT09. Ordinary measurement is frozen after dispatch/loss; measurement_dispute retains the contradiction but has no reviewed accounting effect.

**Eligibility/authority:** an exact original measurement/recorded kg fact, same physical production and allocation identities, proven instrument/transcription/unit error with original scale/provenance and current quantity reconciliation. Source Processor authors production/source measurement findings; a current named destination Processor independently attests any affected accepted/completed or receiver-measured fact. Independent Admin approves the complete vector. A current reweigh alone does not prove original error after real processing, loss or configuration change.

**Bounds/equations:** propose P', affected Dd', Aa', outcome/loss quantities and reservation consequences explicitly, not an arbitrary adjustment delta. Every amended physical measurement needs its own evidence and §4 must hold at COMMIT. Unaffected values remain fixed. Withdraw all affected outstanding R first, then prepare fresh reservations/drafts after approval. Never shrink a true dispatch or completion merely to make P' fit. A downward P' below evidenced D + Ls is an unresolved contradiction unless a complete separately evidenced correction vector establishes otherwise; record it honestly without applying negative stock. Reject partial application of an inconsistent vector.

An upward P' is a correction to the same production, **not another production row**. It may increase V only if the difference is proved physically present on site, unallocated and part of that original production. If the corrected difference was already dispatched, reflect its proved dispatch/receipt vector instead, with no source availability increase. Otherwise retain investigation/pending measurement status; do not mint an available balancing quantity. Actual additional output uses the existing explicit recorded-output workflow within its authorized boundaries; post-closure additional production is not authorized here.

Original dispatch/acceptance/completion records, requested kg and physical custody events remain. Append effective mass revisions, exposing raw and effective totals with review references. Accepted/completed mass may be restated **only for a proven measurement error of the same already acknowledged physical allocation**, with destination signature and fresh evidence applicability; no new possession, newly treated material or changed outcome is inferred. A larger restated dispatch with no proved receipt keeps its additional amount in U. A corrected canonical drive mass restates the entire one-unit production/requested-allocation/dispatch/acceptance/outcome vector consistently; unit membership, origin and one-dispatch uniqueness do not change.

**Fresh steps/closure:** recompute actual output/input balance and retain original input commitment/membership. An evidenced batch input measurement may be corrected only as a measured fact with source proof covering all original inputs; never change frozen original input membership or silently replace its commit record. Apply the same original tolerance formula to the corrected effective input; original balance remains visible. Require fresh completeness/reconciliation and explicit downstream evidence applicability. Open exact dependent obligations for completed facts affected by restatement; do not call them newly performed work. Real material variance/loss is not a scale-error correction. Post-closure limits are in §7.

**Invariants/verification:** single current revision per exact fact, atomic multi-fact vector, nonnegative conserved availability and slice bounds; no duplicate production, hidden input changes or raw-SUM bypass. Test upward/downward/zero retirement/precision/overflow, gross-tare errors, fully dispatched/accepted/completed vectors, unsupported loss-as-error and concurrent correction/dispatch/fulfillment (DR04).

### E — Same-destination named-recipient succession

**References/blocker:** FR25–FR29/FR34; BR04–BR06/BR12/BR14; UC14/UC16, S12/S14, AT02/AT06/AT09. Immutable shipment freezes original membership/version and actor; revoked scope cannot act, and reassigning a general membership cannot repair that shipment.

**Eligibility/authority:** same immutable destination party and exact output/profile namespace, one named active capable Processor there; no different destination, external stand-in or change of owner. Admin opens/coordinates; assigned source/destination staff provide reason, unresolved quantities and original/current scope evidence; independent Admin approves. If no current destination Processor participates, remain held. A newer review within the same permanent profile namespace must be explicitly adopted with fresh review, not automatically revive old preparation.

**Sequence/effects:** append reviewed proposed successor generation; proposed successor explicitly selects **Accept recipient responsibility** under current scope. Only that signed acceptance activates the new effective actor/profile generation. It confers action responsibility, not physical possession. Historical actor/headers/acknowledgments remain. The prior actor's authority becomes inapplicable when succession is approved; the interval before successor acceptance remains held. Cancellation of an unaccepted proposal never silently revives the old actor. Reassignment requires another reviewed generation. Renewal for the same currently eligible person after scope/profile drift uses the same reviewed/newly signed generation; restoring old membership never revives its original authority.

For outstanding actual receipt, successor must personally scan/manual-check and acknowledge the exact dispatched quantity they actually receive/control. Admin/old signatures cannot substitute. Where a valid prior acknowledgment at that same facility already exists, do not repeat custody/acceptance kg; successor accepts responsibility and supplies fresh current control/evidence applicability before permitted completion/assurance. Original acknowledgment actor remains the real actor. If material moved elsewhere or prior canonical custody is conflicted, hold rather than overwrite it.

Undispatched remainder R is withdrawn through existing source controls; prepare a fresh shipment for that remainder with fresh profile/recipient, never replay dispatches. The old dispatched fraction stays under its original shipment with new effective recipient generation. For canonical media, amend context-4 *recipient authority* applicability to permit the accepted successor to reference the actual original receipt, preserving its real actor and detachment. Reauthorize/review assurance expressly; do not rewrite the receipt to impersonate the successor. Discovery context 6 follows §6.

**Closure/invariants/verification:** succession changes no quantity/custody/assurance success or historical completion. One active responsibility generation per shipment, live signed successor scope, permanent destination/profile namespace and no authority revival on reactivation. Test before/after partial aggregate acknowledgment, canonical acknowledgment once, pending proposals, revocation after signing, incompatible capability/profile, competing successors and old private links (DR05).

### F — Permit a reviewed discovery-origin drive's first downstream movement

**References/blocker:** FR23/FR25–FR29; BR01–BR02/BR05–BR06/BR09–BR14; UC11/UC14/UC16, S11/S12/S14, AT05–AT06/AT09. Phase 15 and canonical shipment/receipt guards currently require on-site control/real detachment, so a discovery output cannot draft/reserve/dispatch or obtain recipient context 4.

**Eligibility/authority:** one approved P15 discovery supplement and its exact canonical HDD/SSD output; known source parent/owner/control and current source custody; measured reconciled batch, complete identity/provenance/media obligations, supported satisfied current assurance and no destruction, safety/association/work/transfer hold. Source capable Processor performs an explicit **Review discovery-output movement eligibility** proposal; independent Admin approves its exact context. This is not post-dispatch discovery supplementation and cannot resolve unproven provenance.

**Effects/fresh steps:** approve only the typed origin/movement applicability, then use ordinary fresh draft/reservation/actual dispatch and the named destination's actual whole-unit receipt. No direct stock movement from a review. Source context 5 becomes non-actionable at dispatch, including recognized previously standalone media with another historical origin. All identity, owner, public label, encrypted identifier, first receipts, custody history, supplement and assurance remain. The batch's original input/detachment manifest is unchanged. See §6 for reciprocal custody/provenance and recipient context 6.

**Closure/invariants/verification:** no fabricated detachment/first receipt; one production/unit/dispatch, actual acknowledgment then distinct current controlled-storage outcome only. Unknown/failed/destruction evidence blocks clearance. Verify new and recognized origins, denied cross-owner/facility, historical assurance revalidation without invented repeat work, dispatch invalidation, no second dispatch/re-parenting/owner return and root rollback (DR06).

### G — Revalidate exact downstream dependent impacts and later contradictions

**References/blocker:** FR26/FR28–FR29; BR09/BR12/BR14/BR18–BR19; UC14/UC16, S12/S14, AT09–AT11. Existing impacts lack downstream fulfillment; historical closure cannot be erased and later assurance/measurement contradiction blocks current status.

**Eligibility/authority:** exact affected dispatch, accepted receipt, completion or closure event and connected physical subjects, not all history marked resolved at once. Source/destination technical findings as appropriate; independent Admin approves continuity and independently verifies fulfillment. Actual named recipient alone supplies any genuinely outstanding acknowledgment or new completion evidence.

**Effects/sequence:** use the existing continuity → revalidation_pending → fulfilled mechanism at the established epoch. Add typed downstream proof templates for conserved accounting, preserved receipt/control, outcome evidence applicability and current closure obligations. Continuity alone grants only those preparatory reviews; no dispatch, stock, custody or clearance effect. Exception: a reviewed A/E recovery may name the **exact already-dispatched outstanding claim** for the ordinary recipient's real acknowledgment while dependent revalidation is pending. This explicit P16 permit still requires all independent safety/identity/profile/current-accounting gates and atomically validates the exact approved claim. It cannot authorize new dispatch, unrelated receipt, completion or an Admin acknowledgment. Without this narrow allowlist, requiring a receipt as proof while blocking every real acknowledgment would deadlock recovery.

Retained true completed work/receipt/outcome requires explicit current evidence applicability, not replay. Fulfillment checks exact accounting revision, current recipient/control, actual new receipt if required, live assurance/evidence and all relevant independent obligations. Reviewing one incident cannot discharge a different original target. Exclude only the current review's own derived proof rows from its before/after dependency fingerprint; retain every physical fact, other correction, source generation and actor signature. No self-bumping epoch loop or role-restoration revival. A newer failure/context change opens/reblocks current obligations without rewriting fulfilled records.

A proven erroneous completion claim may become ineffective into the same acceptance's pending outcome, retaining its original record and opening dependent exceptions; only separate actual work/evidence can complete it later. A genuinely completed recovery followed by a later real loss is different physical history, not proof the earlier recovery never occurred. Record the later incident/contradiction with its time and applicable obligations; do not fabricate a new in-scope physical workflow or rewrite genuine historical recovery to balance quantities.

Closure consequences, the permitted scope of renewed accounting and unsupported physical contradictions are specified in §7. Verify same and multiple historical impacts, true-versus-false outcomes, independently fulfilled obligations, real acknowledgment recovery, later treatment failure, post-closure revision and scope revocation (DR07).

## 6. Discovery-origin custody and recipient assurance extension

Introduce **context 6: downstream-accepted batch-discovery HDD/SSD**. Do not route discoveries through context 3/4 or insert a fictitious detachment. Context 5 remains the on-site discovery authorization; context 4 remains detached-origin recipient assurance, with the explicit E successor-authority extension only.

Canonical shipment header carries an exclusive provenance reference: a real existing detachment OR the exact discovery supplement/output plus reviewed F applicability. For discovery, freeze actual current medium subject/custody versions and last event, batch/source parent, supplement/technical observer and review versions, original/reused origin, owner, production/measurement and source assurance. For recognized media retain their genuine earlier origin/receipt; use the discovery-output association to establish this batch movement's provenance, not a rewritten origin. Do not assert a detachment-based source version formula for a discovery.

At actual dispatch: the only current whole-unit reservation is consumed once; source authority is stale immediately. Last confirmed custody stays historical source until actual recipient acceptance; in-transit/unavailable status blocks on-site treatment/work/re-parenting/return. At actual acceptance: named current responsible recipient, correct manual/QR identity, exact one-unit mass, current eligible destination and source context, physical evidence and no conflicting control; append one physical downstream_accepted event and reciprocal subject/custody version increment. Extend the existing custody/receipt guards only for that complete supported provenance chain. No first_receipts row, detachment, new subject/storage facet or ownership change.

Context 6 cites real accepted receipt, discovery ID and original review, batch/source parent, frozen RECYCLE source decision, current recipient responsibility generation and actual facility control. Require exact HDD/SSD capabilities, no unresolved safety/identity/destruction/impact restriction and valid accounting. Source parent remains terminally dismantled; do not demand a fabricated new parent inspection, placement or receipt to satisfy the predicate. Current source/batch truth and original approved supplement remain dependencies, while transient on-site-only predicates no longer pretend the received medium remains at source. Do not invalidate a truthful supplement's history merely because its authorized medium later moves.

Reuse media_authorizations, sanitization_records/current and unchanged [method policy](../app/Support/Sanitization/Policy.php). Explicit recipient applicability review may revalidate justified same-medium historical execution/verification evidence against context 6; authorization, discovery or an uploaded certificate alone never establishes success. New failed/incomplete/unverifiable attempts and destruction concerns retain their effects. No destructive commands/access to hardware. Supported stored surviving media remain data-bearing YES forever. Completion is only supported controlled_storage with current assurance, never reuse/recovery/destruction/release. Loss cannot satisfy context 6 through an invented receipt or controlled-storage record.

## 7. Closure, contradictions and unresolved cases

Ordinary new closure requires complete effective production/balance within unchanged tolerance; zero source A/R; every dispatched slice either actually accepted with current supported final outcome or approved quantified non-media loss; no F/S/Q/U, pending completion, missing evidence or independent obligation. Each surviving drive needs supported current actual control and assurance/accountability outcome. Lost/uncontrolled/destroyed/unknown media or unsupported disposition block safe closure. ## 10. Concrete authorization decisions before implementation

Recommend approving these four bounded extension groups together, through append-only CR-BATCH-01 / CR-MEDIA-01 / CR-RESTRICTION-01 entries:

1. **Typed outcome/incident recovery (A–C/G):** errors retire only proved claim slices; newly located same-destination material takes a separately evidenced outcome continuation and actual acknowledgment; safe aggregate damage needs separate completion; quantified non-media loss may support distinct non-recovery closure, while uncontrolled/lost media cannot.
